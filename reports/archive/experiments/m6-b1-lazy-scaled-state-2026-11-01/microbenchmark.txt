Manual isolated f32 scaled-state setup benchmark
===============================================

Command:
  taskset -c "$RUSTSHT_Q1_CPUSET" cargo test --release --features parallel --lib \
    benchmark_b1_f32_scaled_state_setup_vs_lazy_ordinary_block \
    -- --ignored --nocapture

Set `RUSTSHT_Q1_CPUSET` to the selected benchmark core before running. Inputs:
32 representative nonzero latitude lanes; 20,000 setup iterations per
sample, two warmups and nine measured samples. Source is the test-only
`analysis/tests/lazy_state_bench.rs` module.

Result on 2026-11-01:
  eager F32ScaledAnalysisState setup median: 66.10 ns/state
  eager sample range: 62.14–66.85 ns/state
  lazy `None` block median: 0.21 ns/block

The benchmark isolates initialization cost; whole-transform measurements are
the authority for impact. On the full q1 screen the median saving was 0.416 ms,
consistent with avoiding this setup on ordinary blocks. It does not explain the
remaining scaled recurrence and accumulation cost.
