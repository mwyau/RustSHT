The two-vector suffix path already handles most ordinary updates, so suffix
pairing alone has limited remaining headroom. Some scaled-prefix vector steps
have every lane at scale <= -3, which bounds each restored f32 basis value to
zero under the current normalization threshold. This count does not justify
skipping accumulation: multiple individually tiny terms can combine into a
representable sum. Any optimization must retain or safely bound those
contributions.

The isolated probe passed (1 passed, 0 failed, 0 ignored in the selected run);
`cargo fmt --all -- --check` passed. It makes no timing or performance claim.
