# B2 reflected dual-channel synthesis

## Decision

Historical intermediate B2 candidate; the final production implementation is not included in this evidence-only commit. One shared generic reflected Legendre recurrence accumulates both spin-1 scalar channels, removing the duplicate recurrence while keeping native f32/f64 coefficients, recurrence, SIMD, spectra, FFT and maps. The AVX2 f32 route reports eight lanes. The paired route is limited to child scalar `lmax <= 721`; higher bandlimits retain the established single-channel route to protect T1023 behavior.

This does not complete parity. In the 15-pair candidate screen, full q1 passed all three measured ratios, but full q8 and both reduced-order cells still missed at least one gate. The guarded source was then screened for three pairs per T720 cell. Its reduced q1/q8 f32 ratios remain clear gaps, and its protected T1023 timing remains inconclusive.

T720 f32 output matched the parent bit for bit in the candidate screen, with relative L2 against DUCC f32 of `2.78e-4` full and `2.71e-4` reduced; repeat/reset checks passed.

## Protected T1023 f64 control

Three paired whole-transform runs per q used the same GL T1023 fixture. Guarded candidate/parent median ratios were `1.015` q1 and `1.010` q8; candidate output was bitwise equal to parent in every pair. These short controls are **INCONCLUSIVE** for a roughly 1% timing change, so they do not establish non-regression. Candidate/DUCC f64 medians were `0.779` q1 and `0.840` q8. The lmax guard preserves the prior algorithm and output above the T720 child degree; rerun the longer protected control before making a non-regression claim.
