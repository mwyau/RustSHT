## Priority finding 1 — independent CC auditor falsely accepts absent validation

**Verified source facts:** In [`audit_manifest.py`](reports/data/ducc-scalar-f64-cc-full-sweep-2026-10-22/audit_manifest.py), `validation_reasons(None)` returns `missing_validation` (lines 84–86), but `validation_rejected = validation is not None and bool(check_reasons)` (line ~290) discards that reason when the record is absent. A group with complete process-pair timing, random outputs and resets, but no analytic validation, can therefore receive `numerical_status="accepted"`. `full_sweep_pass` (lines ~447–451) checks rejected counts but does not require each expected group to have accepted validation. This differs from [the runner's `dense_sweep_coverage`](scripts/benchmark_ducc_full_transform_parity.py), which marks absent validation `comparison_only`. The independent auditor also checks that *some* roundtrip and basis `relative_l2` values exist, not that each required backend/mode record is present, finite and within tolerance. Thus these statuses can produce **false acceptance of an incomplete scientific validation record**.

**Required correction:** Treat missing/malformed/nonfinite/incomplete required validation as `incomplete` or `rejected` under one explicit consistent policy, **never accepted**. Require every expected group to have all mandatory backend/mode-specific roundtrip, basis, adjoint, edge, reset and finite fields, with existing tolerances applied individually. Keep coverage, numerical acceptance and timing quality as separate machine-readable dimensions. `full_sweep_pass` must require every expected group to be complete and numerically accepted, as well as meeting the documented timing-qualification policy. Do not equate recorded random-output agreement with analytic validation.

## Priority finding 2 — checkpoint can turn a validation exception into a completed pair

**Verified source facts:** In [`benchmark_ducc_full_transform_parity.py`](scripts/benchmark_ducc_full_transform_parity.py), after `compare_validation(...)` raises, the exception branch records `validation_error` but calls `save_pair_checkpoint(True)` (lines ~1228–1247) and continues. During resume, any checkpoint pair marked `complete` is loaded/skipped (lines ~1010–1034) even if its analytic validation was not recorded. In conjunction with finding 1, this can conceal missing analytic tests while preserving ostensibly complete timing samples.

**Required correction:** A validation-error checkpoint must retain evidence of its failure, **not** mark the pair validation-complete. On resume, rerun only the missing validation/required pair when safe, or explicitly preserve it as failed/incomplete; do not discard historical samples, falsify frozen-binary/source hashes, relax shard-merger identity checks, or rerun the entire campaign unnecessarily. Keep `duration_window_status` independent from sample-count qualification and label 20–100 ms short-kernel target violations as uncalibrated/qualified-with-caveat according to the existing benchmark contract, not silently good measurements.
