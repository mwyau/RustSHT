# Native-f32 reflected-ring synthesis candidate — rejected

**Decision:** keep this candidate out of production. It substantially reduced
the four GL T720 scalar-synthesis whole-call times, but the exact post-layout
candidate regressed the protected reduced-mmax f32 scalar-analysis q8 cell by
4.3% in all 15 paired runs.

## Whole-transform synthesis screen

Three interleaved parent/candidate/DUCC pairs were collected for each cell
from the exact post-layout source. Times below are medians in milliseconds;
candidate/parent and candidate/DUCC are medians of paired ratios.

| Cell | Parent | Candidate | DUCC | Candidate/parent | Candidate/DUCC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full q1 | 710.758 | 474.269 | 19.946 | 0.6662 | 23.777× |
| Full q8 | 108.306 | 61.632 | 2.662 | 0.5697 | 23.144× |
| Reduced q1 | 213.223 | 151.103 | 9.005 | 0.7087 | 16.770× |
| Reduced q8 | 55.839 | 21.621 | 1.190 | 0.3874 | 18.164× |

All 12 worker outputs were finite and repeat/reset consistent. Candidate-parent
relative L2 was at most `5.04e-7`; maximum absolute difference was at most
`2.75e-6`. Candidate-DUCC relative L2 was `1.72334e-4` full and `2.72540e-4`
reduced, matching the parent to the displayed precision. This is a promising
synthesis-only timing result, not a promotion: the q8 scalar-analysis control
below fails.

## Protected f32 scalar-analysis control

For reduced GL T720 scalar analysis q8, 15 paired runs measured parent/candidate/
DUCC medians of `25.444`/`26.548`/`0.871` ms. Candidate/parent was `1.0430×`
with a 95% paired bootstrap interval of `[1.0384, 1.0455]`; the candidate was
slower in all 15 pairs. Parent and candidate output bytes were identical in all
15 pairs, and every worker was finite and repeat/reset consistent. The result
therefore reflects a repeatable runtime regression in an unchanged analysis
path, not changed numerical output.

The reflected module split was tried to change executable layout. The candidate
binary is 68,112 bytes larger; the unchanged analysis accumulator remains 616
encoded bytes and moved from `0x11a8d0` to `0x1206d0`. Those facts do not prove a
cause. `perf` counters were unavailable (`perf_event_paranoid=4`), and no
matched Legendre-only timing or comparable Callgrind run was obtained. Do not
attribute the regression to instruction-cache behavior without further
evidence.

## Validation and next step

Candidate formatting, workspace/all-target/all-feature `cargo check`,
warning-denied workspace Clippy, and the focused T720 integration test passed.
That test covers full/reduced mmax at q1/q8, f32-vs-f64 relative-L2 and maximum
absolute error, analytic `m=0` modes, a reduced q1 adjoint identity, and
workspace reset. The candidate was rejected before the broader polar,
subnormal, nonfinite, overflow, and SIMD-tail acceptance matrix.

The current production `dev` source was unchanged. Rust CI run 817 at
`d2c1ee98` passed formatting, workspace check, Clippy, Ubuntu workspace tests,
and Python tests; Windows and macOS format/check/Clippy jobs also passed. The
earlier CI lint failures were addressed by `30a8cfb7`.

Next, measure the scaled-prefix transition on the exact current synthesis path
and count useful safe suffix work and later declines. Any new handoff must
reduce the repeated scaled-contribution work itself, preserve the exact fallback,
and include a separately measured Legendre stage before it is considered.
