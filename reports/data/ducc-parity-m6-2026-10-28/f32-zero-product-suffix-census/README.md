# Native-f32 T720 zero-result suffix gate census — 2026-10-28

**Result:** all suffix packets that previously declined at the strict product-normal guard completed when the guard accepted either a normal product or a zero-valued result. No packet declined at a later coefficient, product, sum, recurrence or overflow check. This result predicate does **not** establish that the product is mathematically zero: a nonzero product can round to zero. The q1 timing candidate subsequently failed numerical comparison; see the [rejection report](../f32-zero-product-suffix-q1-rejection/README.md).

The extra fused suffix covers **55.14% / 53.53%** of active GL T720 full-mmax output terms at q1/q8 and **46.77% / 44.23%** at reduced mmax. This adds 28.90M / 35.45M full and 9.42M / 11.29M reduced degree–latitude terms over the strict guard. These are route counters from an instrumented build, not elapsed-time or Callgrind results.

## Decision

The route count shows a substantial increase in fused work, but it did not establish numerical safety and the follow-up q1 candidate failed with large errors. Do not reuse this result-only predicate. A new route test must allow a zero product only when the corresponding recurrence state or coefficient operand is itself exactly zero; products rounded to zero from nonzero operands must stay on the scaled fallback. Re-measure route coverage and verify numerical output before any further timing screen.
