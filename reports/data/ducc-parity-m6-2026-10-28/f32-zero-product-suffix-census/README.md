# Native-f32 T720 exact-zero suffix gate census — 2026-10-28

**Result:** all suffix packets that previously declined at the strict product-normal guard completed when the guard accepted either a normal product or exact zero. No packet declined at a later coefficient, product, sum, recurrence or overflow check. Since the only changed condition was the product check, the previous declines were caused by exact-zero products, not subnormal or nonfinite products.

The extra fused suffix covers **55.14% / 53.53%** of active GL T720 full-mmax output terms at q1/q8 and **46.77% / 44.23%** at reduced mmax. This adds 28.90M / 35.45M full and 9.42M / 11.29M reduced degree–latitude terms over the strict guard. These are route counters from an instrumented build, not elapsed-time or Callgrind results.

## Decision

This exact-zero exception is safe to screen as a bounded candidate: a zero product contributes no value, while subnormal and nonfinite products still fail the unchanged guards. The route count shows a substantial increase in useful fused work, unlike the prior strict guard. Next compare a non-instrumented candidate with exact-parent and pinned-DUCC q1 timings, then q8 and adjacent controls if q1 is promising. Do not promote based on this census alone; run the full numerical, transactional, and protected-cell gates before production adoption.
