# Isolated P02 incremental synthesis screen

This screen directly compares the frozen P01-only binary with the retained P01+P02 binary; no build was performed. It measures full-transform synthesis at T1023 scalar and T2047 scalar, with T511 scalar and spin-1 controls. Each cell has six fresh-process pairs with order alternating. The harness configuration is GL STANDARD f64, `phi0=0.37`, full `mmax=L`, `ntheta=L+1`, `nphi=2*(L+1)`, one requested Rayon thread, requested-only mode, one warm-up and five samples per process. Processes are pinned to one CPU core. `/usr/bin/time -v` records peak RSS and process timing, and every process runs a local CPU-pressure and interference preflight; its raw snapshot remains untracked. CPU-pressure validity was reviewed under the declared protocol; exact transient readings remain local. `run-one.sh` records additional preflights and refuses concurrent compiler/benchmark processes or unrelated processes at or above 8% CPU. The summarizer reports six paired candidate/P01 ratios and a deterministic 95% pair-bootstrap interval, seed 20261017.
