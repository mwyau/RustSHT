# RustSHT performance candidate backlog — recoverable experiments

**Created:** 2026-10-25 UTC. **Scope:** historical f64 spin-0/spin-1 SHT investigations, particularly GL T720 spin-1 analysis, plus deferred cross-workload leads. **Pinned DUCC:** 0.42.0 `42ce8ab5c5d79e0e2fd9af4a09c2ea4693520e1b`. This is an **experiment backlog, not an accepted-performance ledger or approval to change production behavior**. See [accepted performance](../PERFORMANCE_STATUS.md), [rolling progress](PROGRESS.md), [negative experiments](NEGATIVE_EXPERIMENTS.md), [active plan](../plans/f64-ducc-parity-all-cases-2026-10-24.md).

## Policy: do not discard inconclusive but credible work

- **A — instruction win / timing unresolved:** correctness checked, lower *matched* Callgrind Ir or demonstrated codegen improvement; whole-transform timing flat, noisy, or mixed. Preserve a source SHA and A/B recipe. Prefer these over inventing near-identical fresh experiments. Source need not be in production until regression coverage is adequate.
- **B — near-neutral or conditional timing lead:** directionally faster in some workload or parameter but confidence overlaps zero, or meaningful speedup in one cell with inconclusive/negative control. Keep as an opt-in experimental candidate and revisit under controlled measurement or a proven dispatch gate.
- **C — strong measured regression or correctness failure:** archival reference **not a recommended retry unchanged**. Restore only with a *substantively new mechanism*, measured attribution, and tests. Failed correctness/reset is a hard stop, not rescued by lower instructions.
- **D — already accepted:** record separately in `PERFORMANCE_STATUS.md`; do not mislabel an accepted optimization as rejected.
- **Selection rule and owner's tie-breaker:** A reproducible machine-instruction reduction **is evidence of a worthwhile candidate**, even if time gain is unresolved. When matched elapsed measurements across *all affected cells* are statistically indistinguishable and correctness is equivalent, **prefer the fewer-instruction implementation as a tie-breaker**; do not require a provable wall-time improvement solely to keep it as a viable candidate. This **does not** establish runtime speedup, nullify a meaningful q8 slowdown, or satisfy DUCC per-cell parity. Prefer keeping exact source in Git history / an isolated worktree or feature gate when production deployment still has unresolved regression risk. Compare Ir, cycles/elapsed if available, runqueue wait, *q1 and q8*, affected grid/size/thread controls and correctness. Do not pool q1 and q8 or interchange process medians and paired median ratios.
