## Current B1 profile, result and next action

Matched nonzero T720 phase probes put Legendre recurrence plus accumulation at 93.5–93.9% of f32 q1/q8 wall time and 82.4–83.0% of f64; FFT takes 6.0–6.1% and 16.3–17.5%, respectively. A current f32 route probe records scaled-pair accumulation in 20.0% of full-order and 13.3% of reduced-order pair invocations; the other invocations use the ordinary path. The f64 q8 queue-lock probe measured 33.0 μs across 721 jobs (1.3% of its 2.50 ms diagnostic call). These are non-overlapping phase timings and route counts, not hardware-cycle shares. `perf stat`/`perf record` are unavailable on this host (`perf_event_paranoid=4`), and Callgrind instruction counts are not cycles.

The f32-only two-pair ordinary traversal reduces full-call time by 5.0–8.3% in the 15-pair T720 matrix while preserving bitwise-equal output. This supports avoidable per-pair loop overhead: the safe ordinary route handles 80.0% of full and 86.7% of reduced typed pair invocations, and the emitted AVX2 loop reuses a loaded latitude vector across adjacent pairs without hot-loop YMM spills. The f32 V3 function grows 13.7%; f64 machine code is byte-identical. This does not quantify cycles spent in recurrence, accumulation, normalization or exceptional handling. f32/DUCC remains 2.20–2.85x GAP; f64/DUCC remains 1.226–1.310x GAP.
