## Current B1 profile and result

On exact parent `792c8f363ad73cd839a0df73965714b74623ac6e`, Callgrind full-q1 totals are 715,838,219 Ir for f32 and 278,584,228 Ir for f64. The symmetric order group accounts for 94.32%/81.37%, including the fused paired block at 86.28%/59.81%; forward FFT is 5.68%/18.61%, pair preparation 2.96%/7.46%, and factor fill 1.24%/3.36%. These are inclusive call-tree shares, not additive stage costs. Candidate totals are 691,336,065/265,147,211 Ir, reductions of 3.42%/4.82%. Hardware `perf stat`/`perf record` are blocked (`perf_event_paranoid=4`), so Ir is not cycles.

The ordinary two-degree SIMD loop is the measured target. Parent AVX2 code emits four per-vector slice-length comparisons/branches and repeated stack base reloads; zipped iteration emits one loop guard, retains the FMA sequence, and keeps bases in registers. The f32 ordinary block microbenchmark measured 45.34 ns indexed vs 27.78 ns zipped median across nine samples. This is consistent with the whole-call data but is not a substitute for it. The implementation does not alter normal/scaled arithmetic, exponent normalization, recurrence order, FFT or scheduling. Existing route traces count 266,685 scaled f32 pair calls and 159,441 factor epochs per nonzero full-q1 transform; they are counts, not elapsed-time shares. Recurrence vs accumulation vs exceptional fallback and q8 scheduling/memory-system time are not independently apportioned by this profile.

Fifteen interleaved GL T720 parent/candidate/DUCC pairs show f32 candidate/parent improvement in full q8 and reduced q1/q8, with full q1 INCONCLUSIVE; f64 is INCONCLUSIVE in all cells and shows no resolved regression. Candidate/DUCC remains 2.10–2.72x for f32 and 1.03–1.13x for f64; reduced-q8 f32 has wide uncertainty from DUCC outliers. All parent/candidate outputs are bit-identical across 120 process pairs. The source crosswalk, per-pair results, fixture/binary hashes, test-only microbenchmark patch, Callgrind profiles and instruction excerpts are in the [2026-11-02 B1 package](archive/experiments/m6-b1-zip-loop-2026-11-02/README.md).

A three-pair exact-parent control measured f32/Rust-to-DUCC at 2.26–2.79x and f64 at 1.02–1.14x; this did not reproduce the owner-provided earlier timing ranges, so the source/fixture/host-specific measurements are reported without inferring an unexplained performance change. Clean candidate validation passed formatting, all-feature workspace check, strict Clippy, debug all-feature workspace tests (329 unit and 19 integration tests; 41 ignored), release Rust tests, and Python API tests (20 passed). A protected GL T1023/M1023 f64 spin-1 synthesis control showed parent/candidate medians 1.0005 q1 and 0.9886 q8 over three pairs, with exact outputs/repeat/reset checks; this confirms no code-path change but is too small a sample to establish a timing effect. ### Follow-up profile and rejected scale candidate — 2026-11-02

At production source `8560bcae`, test-only nonzero T720 phase probes attribute 91–95% of instrumented f32 and 76–86% of f64 wall time to Legendre plus accumulation. The remaining disjoint FFT phase is 5–9% f32 and 13–22% f64. Route counters show 266,685/89,429 f32 scaled pair calls for full/reduced orders, with nonzero exponent states in 81%/78% of samples; hard recovery records 5,601 scalar lanes per geometry. f64 typed-seed attempts decline 474 full-order and 132 reduced-order blocks. These counts do not apportion cycles. PMU access remains blocked, and neither phase timing nor Callgrind Ir establishes recurrence or normalization cycle shares.

The isolated f32 output-scale exponent-bit shortcut improved a pinned 15-sample helper benchmark from 2.585 to 2.246 ns/value (paired ratio 0.881 [0.855, 0.909]) and was bit-equivalent over 4,096 inputs × 17 exponents. However, three-pair whole-call candidate/parent intervals crossed 1.0 in all eight precision/cell combinations; point estimates were slower in three of four f32 cells. The inlined V3 function grew 5.8%, with 52 extra branches and unchanged FMA count. **Rejected; not in production.** The detailed phases, route counts, raw bounded traces, screen, disassembly summary, patch and hashes are in the [scale-output profile package](archive/experiments/m6-b1-scale-output-fast-path-2026-11-02/README.md).

### No-change f32 normalizer bypass — isolated and rejected

On base `d4cc76bfe22410ec410bfffac7f00d7b677fb928`, the candidate added an early no-change vector branch in the native-f32 pair normalizer. The mixed synthetic normalizer microbenchmark improved 11.6% (107.39 to 94.90 ns/block), while vectors requiring a small-lane scale update regressed 6.7%. The generated f32 V3 body grew 736 bytes and retained its 6,120-byte frame; f64 V3 instructions were identical after address normalization.
