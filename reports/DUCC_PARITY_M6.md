## Current B1 bottleneck and next experiment

B1 native f32's last controlled baseline is 3.59–4.96x DUCC f32; the f32/Rust f64 ratio is diagnostic only. The pre-unroll f64 baseline was 1.18–1.37x DUCC f64. The accepted f64 two-pair traversal now measures 1.242–1.289x DUCC f64 across the four T720 cells, improving the exact parent by 3.6–9.7%. Source-specific paired results, microbenchmarks, disassembly and output controls are in the [two-pair analysis package](archive/experiments/m6-b1-two-pair-analysis-unroll-2026-11-01/README.md). The existing sparse-index q1 profile was 1.244B B1 Callgrind instructions, 15.1% fewer than `47906d09`; Callgrind instructions **are not cycles**. The separate quiet B1 full-q8 whole-binary control was 1.060 [1.039,1.099] versus `95bcbecc`; its cause is unresolved.

At parent `410c9ec`, warm phase probes found recurrence plus accumulation at 94–97% of f32 time and 82–91% of f64 time across full/reduced q1/q8; FFT was 3–6% f32 and 8–18% f64. Full q1 Callgrind totals were 1.239B f32 and 318M f64 Ir. Test-only region runs measured 20.9M normal accumulation, 539.1M scaled accumulation including 409.6M factor preparation, 49.8M recurrence, and 538.8M range check plus normalization including 232.2M normalization. These are separate targeted Ir counts; subset rows are not additive. About 52.9% of full-order f32 coefficient-pair accumulations took the scaled path. `perf stat`/`perf record` remain unavailable (`perf_event_paranoid=4`, no perf capabilities), so elapsed-stage and instruction data do not establish a cycle-level cause. The ordinary Rust f32 path emits eight-lane AVX2/FMA accumulation with four `vfmadd231ps` operations per basis vector and horizontal reductions; DUCC's `map2alm_kernel` is smaller and unrolls two degree updates, but code size alone does not explain the gap. Full source, disassembly, raw profiles, hashes and screens are in the [410c B1 profile package](archive/experiments/m6-b1-profile-410c-2026-10-31/README.md).

Four further f32 experiments were rejected: range-check fusion slowed full q1/q8; direct power-of-two factors reduced factor-preparation Ir by 14.1% but regressed three cells and was inconclusive in full q8; checked exponent arithmetic increased that region's Ir by 4.9%; selective hard-factor clearing lowered it by 0.24% but regressed full-q1 whole-call time by 12–16% over two interleaved pairs. The f64 two-pair traversal is the accepted change. A follow-up found that the earlier f32 nonfinite/reset failure came from an incorrect scalar-tail recurrence-state handoff, not from an intrinsic f32 arithmetic limitation. With that handoff corrected, all 14 non-ignored f32 integration tests pass while unroll is enabled; however, a three-pair screen regressed every cell by 1.5–2.7% with bit-identical output. The f32 setting remains disabled. Patches, counts and earlier f32 screens are in the [B1 profile package](archive/experiments/m6-b1-profile-410c-2026-10-31/README.md); exact f64 pair data and corrected f32 screen are in the [two-pair analysis package](archive/experiments/m6-b1-two-pair-analysis-unroll-2026-11-01/README.md).
