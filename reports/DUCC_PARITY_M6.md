## Profile and next measured work

The new B3 scaled recurrence fusion removes a second scaled-prefix recurrence pass, but nearly doubles the V3 channel kernel's disassembled instruction count and increases stack-based operand references; further B3 fusion needs a measured code-size hypothesis.
