## Objective and two distinct gates

1. **Breadth-first performance milestone (NEXT):** port the existing *optimized f64 mathematical algorithms* into shared, monomorphized native-f32/f64 implementations for **all four** spin-0/spin-1 synthesis/analysis families. The isolated native-f32 scalar-synthesis prototype is the starting pattern, **not** a numerically accepted production implementation. Bring the other **three** families from their measured 20–47x DUCC gaps down to **<=2.0x the matching optimized RustSHT f64 whole-call time**, with **<=1.5x f64 as the stretch target**, in *each* GL T720 full/reduced q1/q8 cell. “50–100% slower than f64” here means f32/f64 wall-time <=1.5–2.0; it does **not** mean 50–100% of DUCC's time. Measure the actual matching f64 times on the same source revision and setup; do not infer the ratio from DUCC timing. Treat misses as GAP, not as an average to offset.
2. **Numerical convergence and final parity (AFTER breadth):** resolve the documented native-f32 high-degree errors; meet independently justified f32 accuracy/adjoint/exceptional contracts, preserve f64 controls and achieve **native-f32 Rust/DUCC <=1.00 per cell** with credible paired uncertainty. Only this second gate can close M6 and replace the existing production conversion sidecar.

**Architecture is mandatory throughout:** native f32 inputs, plan/workspace, recurrence states, SIMD, accumulation, spectrum, FFT and outputs. No f32-to-f64 transform promotion, nested f64 work plan, duplicated f32 recurrence, or f64 compute to hit the interim performance gate. Higher precision is allowed for *small plan-time constant preparation* and diagnostic references, not as a runtime Legendre backend. Share control flow and mathematical kernels with optimized f64; use narrow typed SIMD/scale helpers. This is an implementation-first **prototype** milestone, not permission to ship known inaccurate transforms.

## Baseline and evidence

- Production scalar synthesis uses a *temporary guarded f64 child-plan sidecar*: GL T720 full/reduced q1/q8 Rust/DUCC **0.829 / 0.961 / 0.775 / 0.885**. It is fast and accurate on ordinary inputs but violates the final architecture. Preserve for now; do not extend or optimize it.
- The isolated generic native-f32 scalar synthesis with uniform-exponent SIMD measured **1.249 / 1.256 / 0.995 (inconclusive) / 1.082** against DUCC on the same four shapes. High-degree accuracy remains unaccepted; production dispatch was not changed.
- Production native-f32 scalar analysis, spin-1 synthesis and spin-1 analysis are **~20–47x DUCC** in the previously measured 12 T720 cells. These are the new optimization priority.
- [Numerical-causality report](../reports/archive/experiments/numerical-causality-ducc-0.42.0-2026-10-29/REPORT.md), Rust/DUCC trace patches and binary128/long-double references establish the same underlying scalar recurrence, AVX2 f32 FMA matching scalar replay, small avoidable f32 seed/subnormal and cos² setup differences, and high-degree recurrence error. On the selected public single modes, max per-ring scaled error is **3.29e-3** for generic f32 vs **1.19e-7** for DUCC; these are **not** full/random-field accuracy results.
- The pinned comparison is DUCC 0.42.0 at `42ce8ab5c5d79e0e2fd9af4a09c2ea4693520e1b`. Baseline data and exceptions live in the [ledger](../reports/DUCC_PARITY_M6.md). Production source remains at the numerical implementation from `c63cde44`; the diagnostic commit `3bb97c1` added no production numerical code.

## Phase C — Return to scalar-synthesis accuracy, then all-family accuracy

After B1–B3 are instantiated and measured, return to the saved [numerical trace](../reports/archive/experiments/numerical-causality-ducc-0.42.0-2026-10-29/REPORT.md) and generic scalar-synthesis route. Separate seed-normalization and precision-aware plan-time cos² controls are complete: the former is mixed and globally negligible; the latter improves selected dense and random controls, but seed and recurrence errors remain and no acceptance pass exists. The dense control shows the f32 recurrence remains dominant when rounded coefficient pairs are held fixed; the random A/B shows a smaller aggregate improvement with pixel-level regressions. Keep each correction isolated until an independent accuracy target is established. **Current sequencing:** the three first-pass candidates are measured; B1 scalar analysis next needs the shared fused analysis algorithm, B2 timing passes but still needs accuracy and the protected f64 T1023 rerun, and B3 needs to reduce its measured scaled-lane overhead before a second screen. Then continue the cross-family numerical validation before further scalar-synthesis compensation.

Extend independent numerical accuracy to scalar analysis and spin-1 synth/analysis, including harmonic degree/order bands, near-pole/equator, Fourier stages, adjoints, exceptional magnitudes and independent references. Distinguish deliberate native-f32 rounding from invalid numerical behavior. No private bitwise backward compatibility is necessary, but public API correctness and numerical contracts remain mandatory. B2's interim timing PASS does not close numerical validation or final DUCC parity; B1 and B3 remain performance GAPs.

Only after correctness and final per-cell DUCC acceptance: make coherent production promotions, remove the nested f64 child plan, conversion workspace, old private f32 transform implementations and redundant compatibility machinery. **Do not remove the current working production routes prematurely.**
