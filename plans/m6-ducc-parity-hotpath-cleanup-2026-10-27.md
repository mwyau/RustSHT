## Objective and two distinct gates

1. **Breadth-first performance milestone:** port the existing *optimized f64 mathematical algorithms* into shared, monomorphized native-f32/f64 implementations for **all four** spin-0/spin-1 synthesis/analysis families. The isolated native-f32 scalar-synthesis prototype is the starting pattern, **not** a numerically accepted production implementation. Bring the other **three** families from their measured 20–47x DUCC gaps down to **<=2.0x the matching optimized RustSHT f64 whole-call time**, with **<=1.5x f64 as the stretch target**, in *each* GL T720 full/reduced q1/q8 cell. “50–100% slower than f64” here means f32/f64 wall-time <=1.5–2.0; it does **not** mean 50–100% of DUCC's time. Measure the actual matching f64 times on the same source revision and setup; do not infer the ratio from DUCC timing. Treat misses as GAP, not as an average to offset. Bounded misses are recorded as family-specific blockers so work can advance; they do not count as passes.
2. **Numerical convergence and final parity (AFTER breadth):** resolve the documented native-f32 high-degree errors; meet independently justified f32 accuracy/adjoint/exceptional contracts, preserve f64 controls and achieve **native-f32 Rust/DUCC <=1.00 per cell** with credible paired uncertainty. Only this second gate can close M6 and replace the existing production conversion sidecar.

**Architecture is mandatory throughout:** native f32 inputs, plan/workspace, recurrence states, SIMD, accumulation, spectrum, FFT and outputs. No f32-to-f64 transform promotion, nested f64 work plan, duplicated f32 recurrence, or f64 compute to hit the interim performance gate. Higher precision is allowed for *small plan-time constant preparation* and diagnostic references, not as a runtime Legendre backend. Share control flow and mathematical kernels with optimized f64; use narrow typed SIMD/scale helpers. This is an implementation-first **prototype** milestone, not permission to ship known inaccurate transforms.

## Baseline and evidence

- Production scalar synthesis uses a *temporary guarded f64 child-plan sidecar*: GL T720 full/reduced q1/q8 Rust/DUCC **0.829 / 0.961 / 0.775 / 0.885**. It is fast and accurate on ordinary inputs but violates the final architecture. Preserve for now; do not extend or optimize it.
- The isolated generic native-f32 scalar synthesis with uniform-exponent SIMD measured **1.249 / 1.256 / 0.995 (inconclusive) / 1.082** against DUCC on the same four shapes. High-degree accuracy remains unaccepted; production dispatch was not changed.
- Production native-f32 scalar analysis, spin-1 synthesis and spin-1 analysis are **~20–47x DUCC** in the previously measured 12 T720 cells. These are the new optimization priority.
- [Numerical-causality report](../reports/archive/experiments/numerical-causality-ducc-0.42.0-2026-10-29/REPORT.md), Rust/DUCC trace patches and binary128/long-double references establish the same underlying scalar recurrence, AVX2 f32 FMA matching scalar replay, small avoidable f32 seed/subnormal and cos² setup differences, and high-degree recurrence error. On the selected public single modes, max per-ring scaled error is **3.29e-3** for generic f32 vs **1.19e-7** for DUCC; these are **not** full/random-field accuracy results.
- The pinned comparison is DUCC 0.42.0 at `42ce8ab5c5d79e0e2fd9af4a09c2ea4693520e1b`. Baseline data and exceptions live in the [ledger](../reports/DUCC_PARITY_M6.md). ## Phase B — Port three missing families (breadth screen complete; timing gaps documented)

**B1. Scalar analysis — native-f32 route complete; interim timing GAP.** The shared reflected order-major/fused analysis and paired recurrence execute natively in f32. A fresh four-worker, three-pair screen on the exact `dev` source plus the adaptive/preflight patch and exact normal-scale f32 leaf measures 50.281 / 6.741 / 18.695 / 2.603 ms for f32, 19.529 / 2.788 / 8.272 / 1.209 ms for matched f64, and 14.135 / 2.061 / 5.789 / 0.901 ms for DUCC (full q1/q8, reduced q1/q8). Ratios are 2.579 / 2.416 / 2.260 / 2.158x f64 and 3.557 / 3.271 / 3.229 / 2.888x DUCC; all four cells miss the interim gate. The exact leaf improves its adaptive-only parent by 1.5–2.6%, with byte-identical outputs in all 12 paired runs. Scalar-analysis unit tests (38 passed) and f32 integration tests (14 passed) include analytic modes, adjoints and workspace reset. The earlier independent binary128 probe for the adaptive-only route measures relative L2 `4.9081e-5` for scalar analysis and `4.8309e-5` / `5.3313e-5` for spin-analysis E/B on 11 orders and `l=600..720`; it is not a full-array or science-tolerance acceptance, and the scale-leaf candidate has no new independent high-band pass. The remaining scale-sensitive recurrence/accumulation work is still the performance blocker. Close this bounded leaf experiment and do not repeat it or the prior tile, hard-lane, preflight or ordinary-range cutoff variants unchanged. See the [exact B1 screen and patch](../reports/archive/experiments/native-f32-scalar-analysis-scale-fast-2026-10-30/README.md), [adaptive-only high-band probe](../reports/archive/experiments/native-f32-scalar-analysis-adaptive-preflight-2026-10-30/accuracy-independent-fullorder.json), and [earlier tile package](../reports/archive/experiments/native-f32-scalar-analysis-tile-schedule-2026-10-30/README.md).

**B3. After B1–B3 have native-f32 implementations and measured T720 results, use the saved [numerical trace](../reports/archive/experiments/numerical-causality-ducc-0.42.0-2026-10-29/REPORT.md) as diagnostic context. Defer detailed scalar-synthesis compensation and recurrence tuning until this breadth phase is complete. Earlier seed-normalization and plan-time cos² controls are diagnostics only; the corrected-cos² 208-case residual replay is pre-FFT and not a whole-transform accuracy or cost pass. The [cross-family binary128 package](../reports/archive/experiments/native-f32-cross-family-accuracy-2026-10-30/README.md) includes an added full-`mmax` selected high-band screen: it measures analysis coefficients after the complete longitude FFT and Legendre path, but does not separate their errors. This is not full-array, exceptional, adjoint or science-tolerance validation. None of the candidate screens closes final DUCC parity.

Extend independent numerical accuracy to scalar analysis and spin-1 synth/analysis, including all coefficients at `mmax=720`, near-pole/equator traces, separately captured Fourier stages, adjoints, exceptional magnitudes and independent references. The current full-`mmax` screen covers only 11 selected orders and `l=600..720`. Distinguish deliberate native-f32 rounding from invalid numerical behavior. No private bitwise backward compatibility is necessary, but public API correctness and numerical contracts remain mandatory. B2's interim timing PASS does not close numerical validation or final DUCC parity; B1 and B3 remain performance GAPs.

Only after correctness and final per-cell DUCC acceptance: make coherent production promotions, remove the nested f64 child plan, conversion workspace, old private f32 transform implementations and redundant compatibility machinery. **Do not remove the current working production routes prematurely.**
