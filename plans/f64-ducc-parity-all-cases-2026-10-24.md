## Immediate sequence — high-leverage instruction-gap investigation

1. **Normalize mathematical work first.** For the *same* GL T720 q1 fixture and matched native builds, count active latitude-lane × degree-pair updates in *both* Rust and DUCC, with the same definitions. Break down initialized/skipped prefix, checked-scaled, ordinary, fallback/replay and each channel. Distinguish iterations, active SIMD lanes and real multiply-adds. Record per-class work, exclusive Ir and Ir per equivalent update. Use minimal temporary counters; do not commit generic profiler infrastructure. Without this, do not conclude that Rust's ~3.555B fused Ir against DUCC's ~1.785B kernel Ir is purely compiler overhead.

2. **Resolve the outside-the-loop cost.** For Rust `src/spin_transform.rs::analyze_spin_scalar_reflected_order` and DUCC's `fill_m2a`, `calc_map2alm`, `iter_to_ieee`, `spin1to0` and support routines, gather line-annotated exclusive Callgrind and limited optimized AVX2 disassembly. Produce comparable disjoint buckets for spectrum gather/phase/weights, parity/active-lane compaction, safety/finite checks, scratch init and copying, factor/sectoral initialization, and coefficient conversion. Explain the adapter's 711M Ir and the 44.5M unclassified line bucket. Account for DUCC's analogous work; no double-counting nested or inclusive costs.

3. **Compare recurrence and checked/prefix state transitions.** After equivalent-work normalization, determine whether DUCC genuinely skips more degree-lane work, or whether Rust performs more instructions for equal work. Compare the machine-code loop *of the specific dominant specialization*, not whole-program disassembly; inspect vector FMA, loads/stores, spills, mask operations, horizontal reductions and calls. Both ordinary loops already use AVX2/FMA, and the dominant Rust ordinary specialization compiles out vector range/exponent checks: don't assume these are the gap.

4. **Rank up to three evidenced mechanisms** by credible removable *instruction and elapsed-time* cost. Choose **one** highest-value bounded source change. If repeated reflected packing/preflight dominates, consider a single-pass validated dataflow that preserves checked fallback and all exceptional values; this must differ materially from rejected safety-scan-fusion experiments. If work counts differ, evaluate safe earlier skip/transition. If the gap is instruction cost per equivalent update, target the proven instruction source, not a speculative tile or register-heavy dual-channel kernel.

5. **Validate and classify without losing evidence.** Compare exact parent/candidate/DUCC with identical fixtures and AVX2/compiler settings: disjoint Ir and interleaved whole-transform q1/q8 timing; correctness versus parent and DUCC, full/reduced orders, extreme/subnormal/nonfinite, poles, SIMD tails, scratch reset, adjoints and affected other cells. Separate **correctness**, **instruction-count improvement**, **elapsed-time statistical confidence**, and **cross-cell regressions**. A proven Callgrind Ir reduction with inconclusive/near-neutral timing is a **recoverable candidate**, not a permanent failure; retain source SHA and measurements in [PERFORMANCE_CANDIDATES.md](../reports/PERFORMANCE_CANDIDATES.md), even if production code is reverted to avoid regression. Merge into the production fast path only after correctness and cross-cell performance justification. Keep CI green on Linux/macOS/Windows and Python; preserve 10–12-minute meaningful non-force dev checkpoints. Keep raw profiles temporary; concise findings in [rolling progress](../reports/PROGRESS.md). Do not broaden into FFT optimization without a new cause: its matched Ir is already near DUCC.

**Selection rule for near-ties:** prefer correctness/safety first, then reproducible whole-transform runtime improvement without material affected-cell regressions. If timing is statistically indistinguishable, value a clearly reproducible *meaningful whole-call* Callgrind Ir improvement, but evaluate its size against complexity, binary/code footprint, additional specializations/fallbacks, platform risk, and validation burden. **If runtime and Ir are both indistinguishable, inconclusive or negligible relative to implementation complexity, select the simpler source.** Never accept a complicated route merely because Callgrind Ir decreased by a tiny amount in one fixture. Do not choose a slower path purely for aesthetic simplicity when slowdown is established and material. Keep viable alternatives, source SHAs and reasons in [candidate backlog](../reports/PERFORMANCE_CANDIDATES.md); production verdict is separate from research candidate status.

6. Once the T720 investigation stabilizes, continue other measured gaps by absolute runtime impact, then complete the per-cell all-case ledger.

## Immediate fine-grained profiling and disassembly protocol (owner steering, 2026-10-25)

**Prioritize this audit before another speculative source tweak.** Do not rerun a whole-program Callgrind and infer the hot loop from symbol-level aggregates. The objective is to identify the *specific function, executed basic block, inner loop and register/memory mechanism* explaining the remaining elapsed gap. For the exact GL T720 spin-1 q1 **and q8** fixture, compare current optimized `dev` with pinned DUCC. Prior temporary counters reported Rust ~648.673M versus DUCC ~648.669M packed updates, but DUCC’s q8 counter is a shared non-atomic total updated by parallel workers; treat that q8 agreement and its stage split as provisional until safely recollected. Use dedicated quiet builds/worktrees, `x86-64-v3` AVX2/FMA, saved fixture and compiler identities; do not contaminate production benchmarks with counter instrumentation.

1. **Collect disjoint inclusive/exclusive attribution:** `analysis_spin_into`, `analyze_spin_order_group`, `analyze_spin_scalar_reflected_order`, `try_fused_spin_scalar_analysis_pair_block`, ordinary `FusedScalarAnalysisPair` variants, checked-scaled `FusedCheckedScaledAnalysisPairChannels` variants, input safety, factor fill, scratch zeroing, parity packing and `scalar_to_spin1_with_epsilon`. Align DUCC `leg2alm_internal`, `fill_m2a`, `calc_map2alm`, `iter_to_ieee`, `map2alm_kernel`, `spin0to1` and `map2leg`. Account for nested function costs and the whole-worker residual *exactly once*. Latest q8 evidence: selected recurrence **3.381B vs 1.785B Ir**; outside those selected symbols **2.542B vs 1.680B**; the boundaries require normalization before labeling savings.
2. **Rank executed PCs and source lines:** use Callgrind per-line/per-instruction annotation with debug symbols and optimized `objdump`/`llvm-objdump` disassembly, source/assembly mapping via symbol and address. For each dominant function report at least its highest-cost **basic blocks/loops**, their execution counts, operations/branches, exclusive Ir, per-lane-degree normalized cost, and contribution to the **whole transform**. Separate actual FFT, spectrum staging, weighted reflected parity, scalar-to-spin conversion, ordinary suffix and scaled-prefix initialization.
3. **Compare equivalent inner loops assembly side-by-side:** count executed `vfmadd`/`vmulpd`, loads/stores, GPR stack reloads, YMM spills, shuffle/horizontal reductions, safety masks, exponent/finite checks, bounds-panic-edge branches and pointer/loop-condition branches. Distinguish instruction-count changes from µop/dependency, latency, cache or bandwidth costs. Rust ordinary ~3.77 Ir/update vs DUCC kernel ~2.75 is a proxy, **not an ISA-level apples-to-apples statement until scopes match**. Work-unit counting should distinguish prefix/ordinary/suffix and component channels.
4. **Use causal microbenchmarks only to resolve a ranked hotspot:** create a minimal matched block/inner-loop probe in temporary worktrees when the whole-kernel profile cannot establish attribution. Keep work, loads, checksum sinks, inlining, benchmark protocol and compilation comparable. Hardware `perf` may be unavailable; do not present Callgrind Ir as cycles.
5. **Output a short evidence-based shortlist:** top **three concrete source functions/loops** with exact Rust/DUCC location, relevant PC/ASM mechanism, removable instruction upper bound, plausible runtime leverage, confidence/limits, and one specifically scoped implementation idea each. Choose the top candidate for a bounded test and record A/B results and provenance. **Do not optimize only a helper because it shows a 95% self-cost improvement if it contributes <1% of total.**
6. **Retain inconclusive improvements as candidates:** read [PERFORMANCE_CANDIDATES.md](../reports/PERFORMANCE_CANDIDATES.md) and the [historical-method coverage register](../reports/ARCHIVE_METHOD_AUDIT_2026-10-25.md) before repeating old experiments. The audit fetched/index-scanned 170 archived experiment/milestone/plan files plus separately indexed early M4/M5 milestone history; remaining gaps concern raw benchmark provenance and unexamined commit diffs, not permission to declare all older methods exhausted. If an idea reduces matched whole-program Ir and passes numerical tests but the timing CI overlaps parity, log **Ir win/time inconclusive** and recoverable source SHA; do not call it permanently rejected. If one cell slows or evidence is strongly adverse, leave production unchanged and note explicit risk. Accept or revert the production *patch* separately from disposition of the *idea*.

The all-cell parity acceptance criterion is **unchanged**. An instruction win is useful evidence and a viable backlog candidate, **not itself a per-cell timing PASS**. **When correctness is equivalent and all affected cell timings are indistinguishable within credible uncertainty, weigh meaningful reproducible whole-program Callgrind Ir savings against maintainability and prefer simpler code if instruction savings are negligible or uncertain**, keeping explicit watch for q8 regressions and future compiler/CPU effects. Report and archive exact parent, candidate, DUCC, workload, sample method, CI and CPU-contention telemetry.
