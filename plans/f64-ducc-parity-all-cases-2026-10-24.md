## Immediate sequence — high-leverage instruction-gap investigation

1. **Normalize mathematical work first.** For the *same* GL T720 q1 fixture and matched native builds, count active latitude-lane × degree-pair updates in *both* Rust and DUCC, with the same definitions. Break down initialized/skipped prefix, checked-scaled, ordinary, fallback/replay and each channel. Distinguish iterations, active SIMD lanes and real multiply-adds. Record per-class work, exclusive Ir and Ir per equivalent update. Use minimal temporary counters; do not commit generic profiler infrastructure. Without this, do not conclude that Rust's ~3.555B fused Ir against DUCC's ~1.785B kernel Ir is purely compiler overhead.

2. **Resolve the outside-the-loop cost.** For Rust `src/spin_transform.rs::analyze_spin_scalar_reflected_order` and DUCC's `fill_m2a`, `calc_map2alm`, `iter_to_ieee`, `spin1to0` and support routines, gather line-annotated exclusive Callgrind and limited optimized AVX2 disassembly. Produce comparable disjoint buckets for spectrum gather/phase/weights, parity/active-lane compaction, safety/finite checks, scratch init and copying, factor/sectoral initialization, and coefficient conversion. Explain the adapter's 711M Ir and the 44.5M unclassified line bucket. Account for DUCC's analogous work; no double-counting nested or inclusive costs.

3. **Compare recurrence and checked/prefix state transitions.** After equivalent-work normalization, determine whether DUCC genuinely skips more degree-lane work, or whether Rust performs more instructions for equal work. Compare the machine-code loop *of the specific dominant specialization*, not whole-program disassembly; inspect vector FMA, loads/stores, spills, mask operations, horizontal reductions and calls. Both ordinary loops already use AVX2/FMA, and the dominant Rust ordinary specialization compiles out vector range/exponent checks: don't assume these are the gap.

4. **Rank up to three evidenced mechanisms** by credible removable *instruction and elapsed-time* cost. Choose **one** highest-value bounded source change. If repeated reflected packing/preflight dominates, consider a single-pass validated dataflow that preserves checked fallback and all exceptional values; this must differ materially from rejected safety-scan-fusion experiments. If work counts differ, evaluate safe earlier skip/transition. If the gap is instruction cost per equivalent update, target the proven instruction source, not a speculative tile or register-heavy dual-channel kernel.

5. **Validate or reject before the next mechanism.** Compare exact parent/candidate/DUCC with identical fixtures and AVX2/compiler settings: disjoint Ir and interleaved *whole-transform* q1/q8 timing; correctness versus parent and DUCC, full/reduced orders, extreme/subnormal/nonfinite, poles, SIMD tails, scratch reset, adjoints and affected other cells. Retain only repeatable gains without material regressions. Revert no-gain work; keep CI green on Linux/macOS/Windows and Python. Preserve 10–12-minute meaningful non-force dev checkpoints. Keep raw profiles temporary; concise source-backed findings in [rolling progress](../reports/PROGRESS.md). Do not broaden scope into FFT optimization: its matched Ir is already lower for Rust.

6. Once the T720 investigation stabilizes, continue other measured gaps by absolute runtime impact, then complete the per-cell all-case ledger.
