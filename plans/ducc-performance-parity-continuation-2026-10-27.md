## Goal / acceptance contract

Match or beat native pinned DUCC 0.42.0 (`42ce8ab5c5d79e0e2fd9af4a09c2ea4693520e1b`) in **warm, whole-transform elapsed time**, for equivalent RustSHT operations and computational work, **without sacrificing correctness or existing per-cell wins**. Primary precision **f64**; f32 is also a performance target, not an indefinite maintenance-only exemption. Measure the **current** post-refactor f32 path before making f32 performance claims. The f32 initial `06221863` baseline is historically slow in some spin-1 cases; those numbers are **not** the current-head result.

**Parity is per cell, not an overall average.** A cell is PASS when Rust is at least as fast as the paired native DUCC reference to credible measurement precision and satisfies accuracy/repeat/reset/adjoint checks. Use `Rust/DUCC <= 1.00` as the target; classify near-1 cases with noisy confidence intervals as INCONCLUSIVE, not automatically PASS or GAP. Keep distinct status labels: **PASS**, **GAP**, **INCONCLUSIVE**, **UNMEASURED**, **UNSUPPORTED/NOT-COMPARABLE** (last only with specific technical reason). Do not hide a slow case behind an average, a different spin/truncation, process-level setup mismatch, or another case's speedup. This target is a continued engineering goal, **not** a claim that all cells can already be made faster. When a gap cannot be closed with available evidence/resources, document the quantified blocker, retain safe source, and continue other ranked gaps rather than claiming completion.

**Matrix:** Both f64 and f32 where DUCC has a genuinely equivalent native-precision path; scalar spin-0 and spin-1 (and supported distinct transforms for both), GL / Clenshaw–Curtis / Fejér-1 grids, analysis/synthesis and their true adjoints S/A/S*/A*, representative T255, T511, T720, T1023, full and reduced `mmax`, exact and relevant oversampled latitude grids, and q1/q2/q4/q8 threading as applicable. Do **not** run the full Cartesian product blindly: prioritize high-impact measured gaps, sample distinct route/shape families, then systematically fill the ledger to support an eventual per-cell signoff. Unmatched precision/representation/work are not meaningful DUCC comparisons; prove geometry, normalization, layout, Nyquist, spin sign, input/output length, output resets and accuracy first.
