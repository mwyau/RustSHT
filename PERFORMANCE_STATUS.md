**Accepted GL over-resolved synthesis route:** the one-line eligibility expansion (`ntheta > lmax`) lets the existing coefficient cache and order-major queue handle oversampled GL. It changes reduction/summation order, so output is not bitwise identical, but measured candidate/DUCC relative-L2 stays at or below `1.04e-12`; no tolerances were changed. On matched T320 full and reduced q8 workers, Callgrind Ir was 1.717B/816M candidate versus 4.487B/2.670B exact parent and 1.751B/784M DUCC. These are identical whole-worker boundaries with setup/validation/batch calls, not cycles. Full-mmax over-resolved T255/T511/T720 three-pair controls improved Rust time by 76%, 62%, and 53% respectively; their remaining ratios are 0.944×/1.039×/1.162× DUCC and are still preliminary. q1 does not enter this cache path (`execution_threads >= 8`). Focused global/local-cache adjoint tests, T1023 bounded-cache/extreme/fallback, formatting and strict Clippy passed. A dense-random T720 adjoint probe exceeded the fixed absolute `2e-9` assertion on both candidate (`1.56e-8`) and exact-parent route (`2.46e-9`); it is recorded as scale-sensitive diagnostic evidence, while the retained high-degree sparse adjoint test passes unchanged.

**Accepted T720 analysis changes:** q1 Callgrind 7.521B→6.892B instructions over eight transforms (−8.4%); exclusive safety-scan 655.8M→119.3M (−81.8%). The corrected scaled-prefix handoff `35541ac3` passes focused extreme, poison, fallback, polar, reference, and adjoint checks. The combined SIMD finite-mask check `70c92d9c` reduces the two finite-mask reductions to one and adds a measured 3.0% q1 / 2.9% q8 gain over its exact parent; analysis output is bit-identical to parent. Exceptional-lane mask iteration `b0aeec6e` reuses the AVX normal mask to visit only scaled lanes. The sequential buffer rotation `ab3ec7da` avoids copying the pre-step basis in sequential Q/U recurrence. `bf938aba` uses the checked AVX2 exponent scaler when all four lanes of a vector are exceptional and every product remains normal; partial, unsafe, and unsupported vectors keep the existing scalar fallback. Latest accepted `bc9e8bb6` combines checked mixed-lane classification and both channel accumulations in one SIMD pass, removing the 256-element temporary.
