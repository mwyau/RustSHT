**Accepted T720 analysis changes:** q1 Callgrind 7.521B→6.892B instructions over eight transforms (−8.4%); exclusive safety-scan 655.8M→119.3M (−81.8%). The corrected scaled-prefix handoff `35541ac3` passes focused extreme, poison, fallback, polar, reference, and adjoint checks. The combined SIMD finite-mask check `70c92d9c` reduces the two finite-mask reductions to one and adds a measured 3.0% q1 / 2.9% q8 gain over its exact parent; analysis output is bit-identical to parent. Exceptional-lane mask iteration `b0aeec6e` reuses the AVX normal mask to visit only scaled lanes. The sequential buffer rotation `ab3ec7da` avoids copying the pre-step basis in sequential Q/U recurrence. `bf938aba` uses the checked AVX2 exponent scaler when all four lanes of a vector are exceptional and every product remains normal; partial, unsafe, and unsupported vectors keep the existing scalar fallback. Latest accepted `bc9e8bb6` combines checked mixed-lane classification and both channel accumulations in one SIMD pass, removing the 256-element temporary. Its hot AVX2 loop executes eight `vmulpd` and eight `vaddpd` updates per vector with no YMM stack spills; matched whole-program Ir falls 1.42% q1 / 1.37% q8. In 15 quiet-core parent/candidate/DUCC pairs it improved q1 by 1.9% (bootstrap 95% CI [0.9693, 0.9918]) and was neutral to slightly faster q8 (CI [0.9670, 1.0040]); candidate outputs were bit-identical to parent in all 30 pairs. GL T511/T1023 q1/q8 controls showed no material regression; T1023 q8 improved 1.8%. Across controls, candidate outputs remained bit-identical and max candidate/DUCC rel-L2 was 5.75e-14. Full local validation passed: format, workspace/all-target/all-feature check, strict Clippy, Rust (306 passed, 37 ignored), and Python (20 passed). The remaining GL T720 analysis gaps are 1.245× q1 and 1.295× q8; this change improves them but does not close parity.
