# RustSHT — current performance status

**Current outcome (2026-11-02):** B1 f64 remains close to DUCC, with full-order q8 the largest current gap; its two bounded hypotheses were neutral or noisy. B3 f64 remains 1.095–1.139× DUCC at the latest current-source screen. A no-debug Callgrind profile matches the official V3 leaf and puts Rust/DUCC simulated Ir at 1.345–1.363× whole-call and 1.455–1.551× for the fused analysis block. Simulated Rust branch and D1 miss rates are higher, while last-level misses are similar; these events are not cycle attribution. The first debug-info profile changed codegen and is superseded. A 15-pair fast-range hoist did not resolve a whole-call gain. The sampled timer was too intrusive, and `perf` counters remain blocked. B1/B3 kernel candidates are stopped until PMU-enabled or equivalent cycle attribution distinguishes branch/L1 effects from dependency/throughput limits. B0/B2 are controls; f32 performance is deferred. See the [active plan](plans/m6-ducc-parity-hotpath-cleanup-2026-10-27.md), [M6 ledger](reports/DUCC_PARITY_M6.md), and [progress](reports/PROGRESS.md).

Full timings, pair diagnostics, hashes and the narrow runner reproduction are in the [85f74d6d package](reports/archive/experiments/m6-b3-current-source-85f74d6d-2026-11-02/README.md).

The no-debug Callgrind profile matches the release V3 leaf. Rust/DUCC simulated Ir is 1.345–1.363× for complete calls and 1.455–1.551× in the fused block versus DUCC `map2alm_kernel`. The ordinary vector leaf contributes 262.90M full/135.18M reduced Ir; the checked scaled leaf adds 54.74M/19.20M. Simulated branch and D1 miss rates are higher for Rust, but Callgrind cannot measure cycles. The earlier debug-info run changed the leaf and is superseded. A test-only second-channel skip shortens calls but removes required math. Native-leaf selection, duplicate scalar passes, per-degree fusion, ring-phase folding and code-outline variants did not yield a production candidate. `perf_event_paranoid=4` blocks cycle attribution. B3 has reached the bounded stop rule; no further kernel edit is justified without PMU-enabled or equivalent cycle data. See the [current Callgrind package](reports/archive/experiments/m6-b3-callgrind-attribution-2026-11-02/README.md).

An isolated fast-range hoist passed the interval already computed in `transform/spin.rs` to each B3 order group. Fifteen paired GL T720 runs found no reliable parent-relative change: candidate/parent was 0.999 [0.990, 1.009] full q1, 0.996 [0.981, 1.013] full q8, 1.004 [0.996, 1.016] reduced q1 and 1.014 [0.991, 1.041] reduced q8. Candidate/DUCC remains 1.098–1.147×. Outputs matched the parent bitwise. The repeated scan microbenchmark was only 13.4 us on q8, consistent with its small expected ceiling. Candidate rejected; production source remains unchanged. See the [range-hoist package](reports/archive/experiments/m6-b3-order-range-hoist-2026-11-02/README.md).

An additional exact-source ordinary-channel skip screen at `85f74d6d` compares all three accumulation variants on the same deterministic fixtures. Omitting the second channel gives candidate/production ratios 0.757 [0.744, 0.774] reduced q1, 0.781 [0.741, 0.815] reduced q8, 0.837 [0.719, 0.922] full q1, and 0.813 [0.619, 0.968] full q8. Omitting the first channel as well changes the recurrence-only/first-channel ratio by 0–4% by point estimate; only reduced q1 excludes parity. Skip builds intentionally produce invalid outputs, and compile-time removal changes codegen, so these ratios are attribution bounds, not additive per-operation costs or performance candidates. The second channel is required output work. Current `dev` B3 source blobs match the measured source; see the [ordinary-channel attribution package](reports/archive/experiments/m6-b3-ordinary-channel-attribution-2026-11-02/README.md).

The follow-up ring-scale fold had a small reduced-q1 gain only (candidate/parent 0.9944 [0.9905, 0.9984] over 30 pairs); other cells were inconclusive and candidate/DUCC remained 1.088–1.141×. Two code-outline direct-leaf screens were neutral without a tail and 1.180–1.199× slower in the tested cases. These results do not change the stop decision; details are in the [ring-phase](reports/archive/experiments/m6-b3-ring-phase-fold-2026-11-02/README.md) and [tail-outline](reports/archive/experiments/m6-b3-tail-outline-2026-11-02/README.md) reports.

## B1 f64 status

The 15-pair GL T720 results in the B1 zip-loop package use parent `792c8f36` and its zipped-loop candidate; they are not a fresh DUCC refresh at `4a558596`. Those source-specific ratios are full q1 1.059 [1.002, 1.120] GAP, full q8 1.131 [1.097, 1.166] GAP, reduced q1 1.047 [0.988, 1.110] INCONCLUSIVE, and reduced q8 1.032 [0.998, 1.068] INCONCLUSIVE. On exact source `4a558596`, a five-call elapsed phase profile placed 68–81% of the four T720 cells in combined Legendre recurrence and accumulation. A const-specialized optional recurrence step removed an inner-loop branch and pointer reload, but grew the AVX2 leaf 16.7%; three-pair parent/candidate medians were 1.003/0.991/0.987/0.994 and did not establish a material gain. Candidate rejected; B1's bounded attempts are complete. The isolated patch, codegen excerpts, focused test results, hashes and per-call logs are in the [branch-hoist package](reports/archive/experiments/m6-b1-hoisted-pair-branch-2026-11-02/README.md).

Pinned DUCC revision: `42ce8ab5c5d79e0e2fd9af4a09c2ea4693520e1b`. Other grid, truncation, adjoint, and T1023 coverage is not certified at this source. The intermittent native-f32 nonfinite SIMD-tail issue is a separate correctness concern; do not resume f32 performance tuning or weaken tolerances.
